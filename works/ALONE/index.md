# ALONE

for "Web Art as Site"

check it out at [tunapee.online/ALONE](https://tunapee.online/ALONE)

an interactive web experience about the self, isolation, and the desire for community. centered around the *ghe nhựa* — the ubiquitous Vietnamese plastic stool — as a symbol of communal gathering.

[![ghe nhua red](assets/ghe%20nhua%20red.png)](https://tunapee.online/ALONE)

the piece unfolds across several phases: a lone stool sways in silence. clicking it triggers a dramatic expansion — action lines flash, the stool grows larger and larger until it towers over the screen, stacking into a monument to solitude. then, silhouettes of others appear around it, and you're invited to drag stools out from the tower and give them away — one by one — until everyone has a seat.
