# in 26 slides

Made with Ryan Webber and Matthew Blanco for an exhibition at the [New York Transit Museum](https://www.nytransitmuseum.org/program/transit-operas-thu/), in collaboration with the NYU Tisch Operas.

A data physicalization sculpture that depicts 26 years of historical ridership data, telling the story of how New York's MTA became the MTA.

Read Matt's blog post about it [here](https://mattblanco.me/projects/26_slides).
