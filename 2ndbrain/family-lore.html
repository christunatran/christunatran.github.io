family thoughts

01-30-2024

My dad was sent away to live at another’s family’s home as a carpentry apprentice. he had to do work on their house and farm in exchange for a place to stay.

my grandma once had an infection after getting her tooth removed. she asked around the village and family/friends and no one wanted to even loan money. my dad had to send money home at age 16 to help out.

if he were to come to the US at an earlier age, he probably would’ve gone to college. he said he wanted to enlist as a navy officer to give back to his family and to the US, the country that allowed my family to leave Vietnam, where we would’ve probably died in poverty because my grandpa fought on the losing side of the war.

my grandpa was a prisoner of war for 5 years. 5 YEARS!!!

it’s crazy to imagine how close my family was to poverty, from having everything taken away from us by corruption, to where we are now.

Dad expressed that because he’s been through so much struggle, he’s able to put up with any struggle in his life now to take care of his family. Because anything now is better than back then.
