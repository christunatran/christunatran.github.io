11-23-2026 rehearsal MIDI

Nov 23, 2026

Step 1: Turn on the Mac’s virtual MIDI cable

  1. Open Audio MIDI Setup (press Cmd+Space and type it).
  2. Choose Window → Show MIDI Studio.
  3. Double-click IAC Driver, tick Device is online, and click Apply. You should have one port called Bus 1.

✅ Check: IAC Driver is no longer greyed out.

Step 2: Let Ableton listen to it

  1. In Ableton, open Live → Settings → Link, Tempo & MIDI.
  2. Under MIDI Ports, find In: IAC Driver (Bus 1) and turn on Track and Remote.
    • Track lets notes in.
    • Remote lets you map knobs with Cmd+M.

✅ Check: both boxes are on for the IAC input.

Step 3: Connect the page

  1. Open reel_gate_2026_11_23.html in Chrome. It’s now in the xiao_reel_gate_2026_11_23 folder.
  2. Click Connect Arduino, then Calibrate.
  3. Scroll to MIDI out, click Enable MIDI, and allow MIDI access when Chrome asks.
  4. In the dropdown, pick IAC Driver Bus 1. Leave Channel at 1.
  5. Don’t click Start sound. That way you only hear Ableton.

✅ Check: it says “Sending to IAC Driver Bus 1 on channel 1”.

Step 4: Make an instrument track

  1. Create a MIDI track (Cmd+Shift+T) and drag Drift onto it from Instruments.
  2. In the track’s MIDI From box, choose IAC Driver (Bus 1) and channel Ch. 1.
  3. Set Monitor to In.

✅ Check: set Crank to play on the page to off. A note should play in Ableton, and the track meter should move. Raise and lower the rod and the note should change. Turn Crank to play back on: now sound only plays while you crank.

Step 5: Make the crank control volume (CC 11)

Ableton synths ignore CC 11 by default, so you map it yourself.

  1. Press Cmd+M. Ableton turns blue.
  2. Click Drift’s Volume knob.
  3. On the page, click Crank power (CC 11). It sends only that control, so Ableton picks the right one.
  4. Press Cmd+M again to leave mapping mode.

✅ Check: cranking slowly is quieter and cranking fast is louder.

Step 6: Make the crank control brightness (CC 74)

Same steps as Step 5: Cmd+M, click Drift’s filter Freq knob, click Brightness (CC 74) on the page, then Cmd+M again.

✅ Check: slow cranking sounds darker.

Step 7: Choose how pitch is sent

  • Notes or Chromatic mode sends exact notes. Nothing else to set up.
  • Glide mode bends the pitch between notes. The page’s Pitch bend range must match the synth’s pitch-bend range, or the glide will be out of tune. The page uses 2 semitones by default. Check the synth’s setting and make them equal.
  • For smooth slides between notes, put Drift in Mono voice mode and turn up its glide.

Once the basics work, you can map more on the same Cmd+M screen, for example CC 74 to reverb amount instead of filter.
