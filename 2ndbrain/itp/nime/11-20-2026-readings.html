“The Importance of Parameter Mapping in Electronic Instrument Design”, M. Wanderley, NIME-02

  • a violin’s string is both part of the control mechanism and the sound generator
  • different with electronic and computer instruments
    • interface completely separate piece of equipment from the sound source
    • mapping: connecting these two, traditionally inseparable, components of a real-time musical system
      • changes the psychological and emotional response elicited from the performer
  • rate-of-change position, rather than traditional position-only control — exciting to play
    • the volume hand needed to keep moving back and forth, like bowing a violin
    • because of the need to keep moving, it felt as if your own energy was directly responsible for the sound inspiration nime
  • one MIDI slider controls amplitude (volume), another slider controls frequency (pitch)
  • there is a need to come up with better-designed mappings than simple (engineering style) one-to-one relationships
    • multimedia control?
