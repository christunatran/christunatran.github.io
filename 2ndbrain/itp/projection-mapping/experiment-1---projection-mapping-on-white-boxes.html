process

When importing the image, frame them within the quads the same way, and then mask out the individual faces.

Then, map it to the angle of the box.

To do the janky hand-animated chair-stacking within MadMapper, I manually toggled on the visibility of each group of layers representing a chair.

I don’t like how the top viewing angle is warped until it reaches the very top of the box. This is something I’d like to improve.

lessons

I tried to make the chair stacking animation in After Effects, but struggled with masking the various face of the image at different angles. I tried breaking it up into 3 compositions but accounting for their individual warping at the viewing angles was challenging
