brief

Physical prototype

Create a physical intervention or functional prototype that engages with a  system of infrastructure – a device, a sensor, an appliance, an object, a place, a space etc. Your project should interface with an existing infrastructural system (power, water, router, phone, camera, urban systems like waste collection, park, tree, river or other ecological systems). Your project should materially change the terms of this system or context and invert an expectation we have about this system, eg. speed, what is considered internal or external to it, who is the user, what logic or intelligence is used to make decisions. 

Deliverables:

  • Proposal document (Week 5)
  • Physical prototype (or a worked up proposal for a physical work), iterated through individual meetings/crits (Weeks 5–6)
  • Documentation of testing, failure points, and revisions
  • A statement on which method (defamiliarization, inversion, functional diegetic prototype) is doing which work in your project and how.
