ABOUT

I'm Christina. I often go by tunapee 🐟. I'm based in NYC. I'm a graduate student at NYU ITP (Interactive Telecommunications Program, AKA "art school for engineers"). I'm a Vietnamese-American human bean 🫘. I live with two cats named Chilly and Langley 🐈🐈. My interests are summarized with cats, coding, HCI, cultural critique, traditional crafts, new media art, dancing, nature, coffee, sports, reading, hosting gatherings, and taking naps. I'm developing my creative practice and keeping an open mind as to where it will take me. Sometimes I am a barista and hold a somewhat frivolous Sensory Skills certification from the Specialty Coffee Association. Before that, I took an "adult gap year" and backpacked across the world. Before that, I was a part of the F2'24 batch at The Recurse Center where I made amazing friends and became excited about tech again. Before that, I was taking night classes in Industrial Design at LaGuardia Community College. Before that, I was working as a data scientist and consultant. Before that, I road-tripped across the United States and Canada, living out of my car for 3 months. Before that, I studied physics and computational modeling at Virginia Tech. Before that, I was growing up in Annandale, Virginia, playing the violin and making digital art. I have a condition called Myasthenia Gravis, and am in remission as of December 2025. After beating this disease, I now feel like I can do anything! I share this because I want to connect with others who have experienced this rare disease. My life's motto is:

"Start where you are, use what you have, do what you can."
